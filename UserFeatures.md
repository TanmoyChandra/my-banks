# My Banks - App Features

Welcome to **My Banks**, your personal digital wallet and financial organizer! Here are all the features designed to make your financial life easier and more secure, built specifically with your convenience and privacy in mind:

## 💳 Card Management (Debit & Credit)
*   **Digital Card Wallet:** Store your card details securely, including the Cardholder Name, Card Number, Expiry Date, and CVV.
*   **Smart Network Recognition:** The app automatically detects and displays your card's network (like Visa, MasterCard, RuPay) just by entering the card number.
*   **Privacy First (Hidden CVV):** Your CVV is masked by default (`***`) to protect against prying eyes. Simply tap the hidden text to reveal it, and tap again to hide it!
*   **One-Tap Copy:** Need to paste your card details for an online purchase? Use the quick "Copy" buttons to instantly copy the exact Name, Card Number, Expiry Date, or CVV straight to your clipboard without any extra formatting.

## 📊 Transaction Tracking
*   **Card-Specific Ledgers:** Keep track of your spending and payments for each individual card.
*   **Detailed Records:** Log each transaction with its amount, description, exact date, and payee information.
*   **Credit & Debit Categorization:** Easily mark transactions as 'money spent' (debit) or 'payments made' (credit) to understand your card usage better.
*   **Automatic Cleanup:** If you ever delete a saved card, all its associated transactions are automatically cleaned up for you to keep your records tidy.

## 📱 UPI & QR Code Hub
*   **Save Your VPAs:** Store and label multiple UPI IDs so you never forget them again.
*   **Live QR Scanning:** Use your device's camera to scan physical QR codes instantly.
*   **Gallery Import:** Received a QR code in a message? Upload the image or screenshot directly from your device gallery, and the app will automatically read the payment details for you!

## 🏦 Bank Account Management
*   **Centralized Storage:** Keep all your essential bank account details in one place (Account Holder Name, Bank Name, Account Number, IFSC Code, Account Type, and Branch Name).
*   **Easy Organization:** Add, edit, or remove your bank accounts whenever you need to, keeping your information up-to-date.

## 🔒 Security & Privacy by Design
*   **100% Offline Storage:** Your data is yours. Everything is saved directly onto your mobile device. There are no external servers or databases, meaning your sensitive financial data never leaves your phone!
*   **No Internet Required:** Access all your banking details and records even when you don't have an internet connection.

## ✨ Modern & User-Friendly Interface
*   **Premium Look & Feel:** Enjoy a beautiful, modern design featuring high-quality, readable fonts that make viewing numbers a breeze.
*   **Quick Navigation:** Easily switch between your QR codes, Cards, and Accounts using the intuitive bottom navigation bar.
*   **Helpful Menus:** Access additional settings and app information anytime through the convenient side menu.
*   **First-Time Guide:** A welcoming experience to help you get started right away.
